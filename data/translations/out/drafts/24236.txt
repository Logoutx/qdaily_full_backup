## iPhone SE is the first new iPhone to approach the 3,000-yuan price point, and its target is clear — Android

Regular article

“We never intended to sell a low-priced iPhone. Our goal is to sell great phones and deliver a great experience.” In 2013, when Apple launched the anything-but-cheap 5c, Tim Cook explained his position this way.

But Apple’s thinking has changed now. Today Apple unveiled new products at its headquarters in Cupertino. In a launch event just one hour long, it brought us the iPhone SE, priced at only 3,288 yuan — 2,000 yuan less than the cheapest iPhone 6s, yet with nearly identical specs apart from the screen.

Apple is playing the price war game too.

## iPhone SE: 6s hardware in a 5s body

Rumors of a small-screen iPhone have persisted ever since the first big-screen iPhone — the iPhone 6. Now it has finally arrived.

As expected, the phone has a 4-inch screen, though Apple gave it a somewhat baffling name: iPhone SE. At the event Apple didn’t explain what the letters stand for — Smaller Edition, perhaps?

![](/uploads/image/201603/182b14432b7e.jpg)

Surprisingly, it looks exactly like the iPhone 5s. Aside from coming in four colors — black, gray, gold, and rose gold — everything that distinguishes the iPhone SE from the iPhone 5s is on the inside.

![](/uploads/image/201603/bcc4fa8923aa.jpg)

Its specs come close to the iPhone 6s:

- A9 processor and M9 motion coprocessor
- Apple Pay support
- 12-megapixel camera
- Live Photo support
- 4K video recording

In other words, apart from the lack of a 3D Touch display, the iPhone SE’s specs are no different from the 6s, and even surpass those of the more expensive iPhone 6.

Apple emphasized that the iPhone SE is far more powerful than the iPhone 5s — for instance, with a CPU twice as fast and a GPU three times as fast.

The iPhone SE comes in 16GB and 64GB versions, with a starting price of US$399. Pre-orders begin March 24, and it goes on sale March 31, with mainland China among the launch markets.

The iPhone SE fully replaces the iPhone 5s, so the iPhone family photo now consists of the iPhone SE, iPhone 6, iPhone 6 Plus, iPhone 6s, and iPhone 6s Plus.

![](/uploads/image/201603/353126f74a56.jpg)

As for why people would buy a small-screen iPhone, Apple’s reasoning goes: some people simply have a soft spot for small screens, and a cheaper iPhone suits those who are using an Apple phone for the first time.

## The new iPad Pro: an upgrade in the iPad Air line

At the same event, Apple also unveiled a brand-new iPad model: the 9.7-inch iPad Pro.

Consistent with the previous iPad Pro, the new iPad Pro ships with the same-performance A9X chip. Storage starts at 32GB, with 128GB and 256GB versions priced at 4,388 yuan, 5,388 yuan, and 6,388 yuan respectively.

Apart from size, the biggest change on the 9.7-inch iPad Pro is support for the Apple Pencil; the edge of the body likewise has connector contacts, so it can be paired with the official keyboard cover. In addition, the 9.7-inch iPad Pro also has four speakers and fingerprint recognition.

![The 9.7-inch iPad Pro with the keyboard cover and Apple Pencil](/uploads/image/201603/1b7dc66ad13d.jpg)

The iPad Pro 9.7’s screen is also improved over the Air 2: reflectance is down 40% and brightness up 25%. The new display adds a feature called True Tone Display — simply put, an adaptive color function that shifts the screen’s color temperature under different ambient lighting, much like the difference between paper under an incandescent lamp and paper in the afternoon sun.

The new iPad Pro’s camera also got a boost, from 8 megapixels to 12 megapixels, and it now has a flash — a first for the iPad line. Slo-mo and time-lapse photography and 4K video recording are present too. Alongside the camera upgrade came the bad news: the camera lens protrudes, just like on the iPhone 6.

With the launch of the new iPad Pro, the older 12.9-inch iPad Pro also gained a new 256GB model, starting at 8,888 yuan.

Meanwhile, the iPad Air 2 dropped to 2,888 yuan and remains on sale.

![](/uploads/image/201603/a03c99a2efdf.jpg)

By launching the new 9.7-inch iPad Pro, Apple is effectively merging the iPad Pro and iPad Air product lines; going forward there will no longer be a 9.7-inch iPad Air model.

Throughout the event, Apple kept treating the PC as its imaginary enemy. It first noted how popular the 9.7-inch iPad is, citing a cumulative 200 million 9.7-inch iPads sold as evidence. It also mocked the 6 million PCs that have been in use for more than five years without being replaced — how sad.

The iPad Pro’s new tagline on Apple’s website became “Your next computer, now in two sizes.” But despite Apple’s tireless promotion of the iPad Pro’s “productivity” credentials, productivity apps remain relatively limited compared with the PC and the Mac. The iPad lineup may have over a million apps, but apps that aren’t good are still not good — it can only handle lightweight tasks.

At 9.7 inches the iPad Pro is more portable, but the keyboard keys get noticeably more cramped, and the canvas left for the Apple Pencil shrinks.

To reverse the iPad’s decline, a slightly smaller iPad Pro may not be enough.

## The iPhone SE is the cheapest ever, but can small screens still cut it?

This is the cheapest new iPhone in history, and it has only one job: bring more sales to the iPhone.

One of Apple’s most awkward facts right now is that iPhone growth has run its course. According to the earnings report at the start of the year, Apple sold 74.77 million iPhones in the last three months of 2015 — almost exactly the same as the year-ago period following the iPhone 6 launch.

Smartphone growth worldwide has stalled. According to Gartner’s latest report, global smartphone sales growth hit a two-year low, at under 5%.

Since 2015, the iPhone’s share of the Chinese market has also hovered around 25%, without a significant lift from the 6s launch. How to grab market share from Android is the problem Apple needs to solve.

![](/uploads/image/201603/7843f1f8bea7.jpg)

The iPhone SE starting at 3,288 yuan is Apple’s new answer. It may remind you of the 2013 iPhone 5c — a relatively cheap new iPhone that didn’t sell.

But this time the iPhone SE doesn’t want to be an iteration of the iPhone 5c; the SE offers better performance than the 5c at a lower price.

The iPhone 5c wasn’t positioned as “low price”: it debuted at 4,488 yuan, and its specs were a notch below those of the iPhone 5s, which cost 5,288 yuan at the time. The iPhone SE, by contrast, performs almost identically to the iPhone 6s while dropping the price to 3,288 yuan, making it the cheapest new iPhone in history.

![](/uploads/image/201603/7c42c48f7911.jpg)

You could also read it as Apple joining the smartphone industry’s price war, starting to emphasize the appeal of “value for money” in order to stimulate iPhone sales.

Beyond Android, Cook also wants to win over users who bought the 5s last year. When announcing earnings earlier this year, Cook said 40% of iPhone users have not yet upgraded to a big-screen iPhone.

This also holds true in China. Rankings based on app tracking by [Umeng](http://www.umindex.com/devices/ios_models) show that 21% of iPhone users use the iPhone 6, while the iPhone 6 Plus and iPhone 5s tie for second at 15% each.

But the iPhone SE faces plenty of challenges:

**Does anyone still want a small screen?**

Phone users have been trained to prefer big-screen phones. Two years ago, the iPhone 6 broke Apple’s sales records, with 74.4 million iPhones sold in the fourth quarter of 2014 alone — up nearly a third from the previous year.

![Data from PostPC shows that phones with screens above 4 inches account for a growing share of the market and are now the mainstream](/uploads/image/201603/3d4e8edb2d1d.jpg)

Cook said today that 30 million users bought 4-inch iPhones in 2015. What he didn’t say: total iPhone sales over the past year were 230 million units — on average, only one in eight iPhone buyers chose a small-screen model.

**Apps that demand bigger screens are getting more popular**

Today a considerable share of new internet users only go online via phone. PCs and Macs are gradually becoming office tools. For these users, the phone is their TV, e-book reader, and portable game console. And for all of these uses, the bigger the screen, the better.

The same trend is evident in the Chinese market. According to statistics from the [China Internet Network Information Center](https://www.cnnic.cn/hlwfzyj/hlwxzbg/201601/P020160122469130059846.pdf), among the applications most used by Chinese internet users, video, news, and games rank near the top — and all of them are better suited to big screens.

**Without its “premium” halo, is the Apple logo still enough?**

One fact that can’t be ignored: Apple buyers are, to some degree, motivated by brand appeal.

That was one reason the iPhone 5c failed — it openly signaled a “lower-tier” choice: its specs were reduced, and the colorful plastic material only heightened the cheap feel. Although Apple never released sales figures, estimates suggest that in the year after it went on sale in September 2013, the cheaper model in the iPhone 5 family sold roughly [24 million](http://www.forbes.com/sites/ewanspence/2014/06/16/the-iphone-5c-is-not-a-failure-but-this-myth-holds-an-ominous-warning-for-the-iwatch/#255be90a1334) units.

The iPhone SE is far better “value for money” than the 5c, but it still represents the “cheap iPhone.” For those who chase the iPhone’s “premium feel,” it is obviously not the best choice. And with the iPhone SE and iPhone 5s naturally identical in appearance, maybe after buying one you’ll be left with the illusion that “I haven’t changed my phone at all.”

Analysts don’t believe the iPhone SE can shoulder the burden of lifting iPhone sales either. [KGI Securities](http://9to5mac.com/2016/01/24/kgi-iphone-5se-iphone-7/) had previously said that, given the iPhone SE’s lack of any “revolutionary breakthrough,” its sales forecast had been cut from 18–20 million units to 10–12 million. RBC Capital Markets’ forecast is even lower, at around 10 million units.

Perhaps you’d argue that instead of selling the small-screen iPhone SE, Apple could simply launch a phone with lower hardware specs but a screen of 4.7 inches or larger — that is, a lower-end iPhone 6.

But the fact is, Apple places app development at an extremely high priority; releasing a low-performance new phone would only burden app developers. We’ve seen a similar choice with the Apple Watch: from the 2,000-yuan Sport to the 120,000-yuan Edition, the internals of these watches are completely identical — Apple doesn’t want to make things hard for developers.

What we can imagine is how far Apple can go on price. At the current rate of price cuts, by 2017 the SE might well drop to 2,488 yuan and keep selling.

## Finally, Apple also touched on a few other things

In the short one-hour event, Apple also discussed other matters, such as the company’s progress in health care.

ResearchKit, launched previously, works with medical researchers to gather data — preventing disease or documenting the course of treatment to improve patients’ quality of life. Through ResearchKit, users can monitor their conditions and guard against potential diseases.

Apple also introduced a new medical development framework, CareKit. The framework can tailor different treatment plans according to a patient’s condition; its first app will be designed for Parkinson’s patients.

![](/uploads/image/201603/6752da9481d7.jpg)

In addition, Apple announced a 300-yuan price cut for the Apple Watch, with prices now starting at 2,288 yuan. At the same time, new watch bands will go on sale; made of nylon, they come in a wider range of vivid colors.
